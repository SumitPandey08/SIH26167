# Authoritative Sensor Guide: ISRO Earth Observation (Cartosat, Resourcesat, RISAT/NISAR)
**Source:** Indian Space Research Organisation (ISRO) & National Remote Sensing Centre (NRSC)  
**Domain:** Indian Earth Observation Missions

---

## 1. Cartosat Series (High-Resolution Optical & Stereo Mapping)
- **Cartosat-1:** Launched for large-scale stereo cartographic mapping with forward and aft cameras producing $2.5m$ panchromatic imagery and high-accuracy Digital Elevation Models (DEM).
- **Cartosat-2 / 2A / 2B:** Advanced spatial resolution of **$<0.8m$** panchromatic and $2m$ 4-band multispectral. Provides spot scene targeting with roll/pitch agility up to $\pm 45^\circ$.
- **Cartosat-3:** Next-generation optical platform delivering sub-meter resolution of **$0.28m$** panchromatic and $1.12m$ multispectral over a 16 km swath. Used for cadastral surveys, infrastructure auditing, and defense intelligence.

---

## 2. Resourcesat Series (Agricultural & Hydrological Mapping)
- **Resourcesat-2 / 2A:**
  - **LISS-IV (Linear Imaging Self-Scanning Sensor):** High-resolution $5.8m$ multispectral optical imaging in Green, Red, and NIR bands across a 70 km swath.
  - **LISS-III:** Medium-resolution $23.5m$ multispectral optical sensor across Green, Red, NIR, and SWIR bands (141 km swath).
  - **AWiFS (Advanced Wide Field Sensor):** Regional monitoring sensor delivering $56m$ resolution with an expansive 740 km swath and a 5-day revisit cycle. Crucial for national crop inventory and flood damage tracking.

---

## 3. RISAT & EOS Radar Series
- **RISAT-1 / EOS-04:** Active C-band Synthetic Aperture Radar (5.35 GHz) designed to image land surfaces independent of cloud cover and daylight conditions.
- **Polarization Innovation:** Incorporates Hybrid Polarimetry (Circular transmit, dual linear receive), allowing Stokes parameter synthesis for soil moisture, paddy rice monitoring, and tropical flood delineation.

---

## 4. NISAR (NASA-ISRO SAR Mission)
- **Concept:** World's first dual-frequency spaceborne radar satellite.
- **Payload:**
  - **L-band SAR (24 cm wavelength, 1.25 GHz):** Supplied by NASA JPL; penetrates dense vegetation canopies and soil to measure crustal deformation, fault slips, and aquifer depletion.
  - **S-band SAR (9 cm wavelength, 3.2 GHz):** Supplied by ISRO Space Applications Centre (SAC); optimized for crop phenology, coastal processes, and flood inundation.
- **Technique:** SweepSAR technology delivers full resolution across wide 240 km swaths with global coverage every 12 days.
